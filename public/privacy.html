Privacy Policy

Michi 3D · Version 2026-10-09 · In force since October 9, 2026

1. Controller

The controller of your data is ValenciagaPM, reachable at valenciagaprojectmoon@gmail.com.

2. What data we process

Michi 3D does not ask for a password, email, phone number or ID. To play online you sign in with Discord or Google, and we only receive an identifier and your public name from them. We only process:

DataPurpose
The identifier of your Discord or Google account and the name they show for youTo sign you in, approve accounts and block anyone who breaks the rules.
The name you choose when entering a roomTo show you to the other players and attribute your messages.
Room chat messages, with their time and the room codeModeration and general log (see section 3).
The version of the terms you acceptedTo prove your acceptance.
Message reports (who reports, whom, reason)To manage moderation.

We do not store your IP address in our database. To protect ourselves from abuse (mass sending of requests), the server uses your IP only in memory and for a few minutes, to count requests and temporarily limit or block abusive connections; it is not written to disk or linked to your name or your messages. In addition, the providers that host the game (section 6) may process technical connection data, such as the IP, transiently in order to provide the service.

Games (moves, lives, abilities) live only in the server's memory while the room exists and are not stored.

3. Purposes

  • Moderation: being able to review reports of harassment, threats or other content that breaks the Terms.
  • General log of the service: keeping a chat history for a limited period.
  • Access control: approving accounts and being able to block anyone who breaks the rules, without having to block IP addresses.

We do not use your data for advertising, we do not sell it and we do not share it with third parties except as stated in section 6 or when a competent authority legally requires it.

4. Legal basis

Your consent, which you give by ticking the acceptance checkbox in the lobby before creating or joining a room. You can withdraw it at any time by no longer using the online chat and writing to us (section 8); withdrawing it does not affect processing already carried out.

5. How long we keep it

WhatPeriod
Chat messages30 days from when they are sent, then automatically deleted.
The chat of a room where someone reported a message, and the report180 days, so it can be reviewed, then automatically deleted.
Your account recordFor as long as your account exists. You can ask us to delete it. Blocks are kept so that they stay effective.

We may keep data for longer only if a legal obligation or an ongoing investigation requires it.

6. Who we share it with

With the technical providers that host the game and the database, who act on our behalf: Render (server and database) and Vercel (website). Their servers may be outside your country (for example, in the United States), so your data may be subject to international transfer. Beyond them, only the controller will access the logs, for moderation. Discord and Google, which you sign in with, process your data under their own policies.

7. Security

Logs are stored in a restricted-access database, and the administrator query is protected by a key. No system is infallible, but we apply reasonable measures to protect the data.

8. Your rights

You can exercise your rights of access, rectification, cancellation (erasure) and objection over your data by writing to valenciagaprojectmoon@gmail.com. To locate your messages please tell us the room code, the name you used and the approximate date and time, because we have no accounts with which to identify you. We will reply within the deadlines set by law. If you consider that we have not handled your request, you can go to the data protection authority of your country.

9. Minors

Online play is aimed at people aged 14 or over. Those under 18 must have the permission of their parent or guardian. If you are a parent or guardian and believe that a minor in your care has given us data without your authorisation, write to us and we will delete it.

10. Changes to this policy

When we change this policy substantially, the version shown above will change and you will have to accept it again to keep playing online.
